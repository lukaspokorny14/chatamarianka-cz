Composition/framing: taller conifer trees at the left and right edges, lower rolling layers of forest in the middle. Preserve empty truly transparent upper space covering the upper 45%. Edge-to-edge forest band along the bottom, with varied natural tree sizes and delicate silhouettes.
Color palette: muted forest green shades #17392d, #476456, and #7b8d79.
Constraints: actual transparent background and alpha channel, no solid background or paper rectangle. Decorative illustration only. No text, no buildings, no photographic scenery, no logo, no watermark.
